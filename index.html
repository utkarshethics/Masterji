Frequently Asked Questions

Instant answers to our most popular inquiries.

How does the ₹50 tailor booking work?

The ₹50 fee secures an expert tailor's visit to your home. The master tailor takes your custom measurements, records your style preferences, and collects the fabric. Stitching costs are billed separately based on garment types.

Do I need to have my own fabric?

Yes, you provide the fabric when our tailor visits. If you are unsure of how much fabric is required, you can consult our experts during booking or read our online material guides.

What happens if the fit is not perfect?

We offer a 5-day free modification policy. After 5 days, if you have any modification, it will require a new order and new payment.
