Custom Stitching vs Ready Made

Ready-made clothing is faster to buy, but custom stitching offers better control over fit, sleeve length, garment ease, neckline depth, margins and finishing. For blouses, suits, lehengas and occasion wear, custom stitching usually delivers better comfort and a more accurate silhouette.
