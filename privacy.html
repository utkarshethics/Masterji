Privacy Policy

Masterji is a doorstep tailoring and blouse stitching platform serving customers across Bangalore, Delhi, Noida, Gurgaon, Mumbai, Pune, Thane, Raipur and Indore. This page supports transparency around how booking-related customer information is handled.

Last Updated: July 2026  |  Effective Date: July 2026

🔒 Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how masterji.online collects, uses, and safeguards your personal data.

1. Data Collection

To provide our doorstep tailoring services, we may collect the following information:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Delivery address
  • Body measurements
  • Styling preferences
  • Booking history

2. Data Usage

Customer information may be used to:

  • Provide tailoring services
  • Process bookings and payments
  • Communicate order updates
  • Improve customer experience

3. Data Sharing

Customer data is not sold. Information is shared only with trusted service providers, such as payment processors and logistics partners, when necessary to operate our services, fulfill bookings, or comply with legal obligations.

4. Payment Security

Online payments are processed securely through the payment gateway. masterji.online does not store customers' complete card or banking information.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Customer Rights

Customers may request updates or deletion of their personal information, subject to legal and operational requirements. If you wish to make such a request, please contact us using the details below.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

8. Contact Us

For any questions regarding these policies or our services, please contact us at support@masterji.online.
