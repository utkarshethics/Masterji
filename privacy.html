Privacy Policy

Masterji is a doorstep tailoring and blouse stitching platform serving customers across Bangalore, Delhi, Noida, Gurgaon, Mumbai, Pune, Thane, Raipur and Indore. This page supports transparency around how booking-related customer information is handled.

Last Updated: July 2026  |  Effective Date: July 2026

🔒 Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how masterji.online collects, uses, and safeguards your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our doorstep tailoring services, we collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Details: Name, email address, and physical address provided during the booking process.
  • Payment Information: Handled securely via our payment gateway (Razorpay). We do not store your credit card or UPI details on our servers.
  • Service Details: Information about your styling preferences, fabric types, and measurements required to fulfill your order.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To schedule and execute our doorstep tailor visits.
  • To process your ₹50 booking fee securely.
  • To communicate with you regarding your booking status, delivery, or service updates.
  • To improve our website, customer service, and service offerings.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With our assigned tailors to perform the requested doorstep services.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, regulation, or legal process.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. If you wish to exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us on WhatsApp (+91 90197 45931).
